# Student guide
1. Open `/admin/login` and sign in with your email and password.
2. Open **Projects**, add the English title first, then RU and UZ, add tools, turn on **Published**, and save. Your project appears on the public page immediately.
3. When you earn a certificate, open **Certificates**, upload its image, fill in issuer and verification link, publish it. The section appears automatically if it was hidden.
4. In **Profile**, upload a photo and PDF CV. Replace either safely at any time.
5. Use **Seed demo data** to explore; **Clear all data** removes all content for a fresh start.
